How the week works

Four sessions exist: Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B. Run all four if you've got the four days. Only have three some weeks? Drop a different one each time so nothing gets skipped twice in a row.

4-day: Mon / Tue / Thu / Fri
3-day, Week 1: Upper A → Lower A → Upper B
3-day, Week 2: Lower B → Upper A → Lower A

Each session runs 35–40 minutes: warm-up, one main lift, two accessory supersets, a short conditioning finisher, cooldown. That's the whole session — no extra work needed to hit the goal.

One stepper, two people: whoever wraps their main lift and supersets first hops on the Bowflex M6 for their finisher while the other keeps working. Swap when the first person's done.

Quick tools

  • Set-by-set logging, for both of you: every lift and accessory tracks weight and reps set by set, with its own checkmark, same as a logging app — not just one number for the whole exercise.
  • Duration, Volume, Sets: a running tally pinned under the tabs, showing session time elapsed and the total weight moved and sets checked off for whichever day you're on.
  • Rest timer, built into every set: check a set done and the timer at the bottom starts automatically, already loaded to the right length — 2:00 on the main lifts, 0:45 after a superset pair, 0:30–0:45 on the finishers. The "Start rest" button is still there too, for whenever you want to start it manually.
  • Last time, remembered: every set shows what you logged last session right next to the box, and the main lifts add a suggested 80% for warm-up sets.
  • Plate math: on the four barbell lifts, the warm-up and backoff numbers come with plates per side already worked out, based on a 45 lb Olympic bar.

Warm-up (every session, ~5 min)

  • 2 min easy pace on the Bowflex M6
  • 10 bodyweight squats, 10 arm circles each way
  • 2 light ramp-up sets of the day's main lift before the working sets

Rest between sets

  • Main lift: 90–120 sec
  • Supersets: minimal rest between the two paired exercises, 45–60 sec after the pair
  • Finisher: as written per circuit

Reading the effort notes

Same exercises, same order, different dial. On main lifts you'll see a target for each of you — McKinley pushes closer to a true top set, Megan trains with a couple reps held in reserve while the movement patterns get grooved back in. Everything else, pick a weight that's honest for the rep target and adjust week to week.

Strength block Conditioning finisher

Progressing it

  • Weeks 1–4: add weight to the main lift only when every rep of every set lands with good form — small jumps, 2.5–5 lb upper body, 5–10 lb lower body.
  • Week 5: deload. Same movements, about 40% less weight, same reps. Let it feel easy on purpose.
  • Then repeat the block at the new working weights.

Core & Stability, Any Day

For the days a full session isn't happening but your back still wants some attention. Four stability-focused moves, nothing loaded heavy, done in about 15 minutes. Shuffle the whole circuit for a fresh mix, or roll the dice on just the one you want to change.

The Six Week Progression

  • Weeks of Nov 9 and Nov 16: 3 sets each move, planks and side planks held 20 to 30 seconds, Dead Bug and Bird Dog for 8 reps per side. Control first, nothing else.
  • Weeks of Nov 30 and Dec 7: planks and side planks held 30 to 45 seconds, Dead Bug and Bird Dog for 10 to 12 reps per side, a touch more resistance on the Pallof Press.
  • Week of Dec 14: same as above, plus one light hip hinge set, borrow the Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift from Lower A's Superset A, 3 sets of 8, hips only, no rounding.
  • Week of Dec 28: hold everything from the build weeks, keep the Romanian Deadlift, and if it all feels clean you're cleared to rejoin the full Upper and Lower split the week of January 4.
